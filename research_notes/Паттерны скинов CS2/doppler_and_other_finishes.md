# CS2 Doppler and Gamma Doppler phases, plus other knife finishes where phase or seed matters

> **How these notes were made (read first):** The sandbox's egress proxy blocked direct page fetches. WebFetch returned DNS errors and curl got CONNECT 403 for steamdb.com, counter-strike.net, prosettings.net and profilerr.net. Every finding below therefore comes from **search-engine result summaries and snippets**, gathered on 2026-10-19, not from pages I read in full. The cited URL is the page the snippet came from. Prices are **tracker or listing snapshots with no exact date** unless a date is stated. None is a verified individual sale. The report writer should treat the numbers as indicative only.

## 1. Doppler and Gamma Doppler mechanics: is the phase a separate paint index? Which items and wears have them? Does the seed matter within a phase? Who rates them?

### Takeaway
Most sources agree that each Doppler or Gamma Doppler phase (and gem) is its own **paint index / finish ID**, separate from the paint seed. The seed (0–1000) only changes how the colours are laid out *within* a phase. That layout matters most for Phase 2 ("Max Pink / Pink Galaxy") and Phase 4 ("Max Blue / Fake Sapphire"). The phase is visible from the paint index in the inspect data. Seed tier lists are made by the community, differ for each knife model, and often contradict each other.

### Cited Findings
**Paint indexes (finish IDs)**
- Standard Doppler indexes: Ruby 415, Sapphire 416, Black Pearl 417, Phase 1–4 = 418–421. The phase is set by the paint index, not the pattern seed. — [SteamDB.com guide (snippet)](https://steamdb.com/en/articles/cs2-doppler-phases-guide). Note that steamdb.com is a skin site, not Valve's or the well-known steamdb.info. Matched by [CSDROP Wiki](https://wiki.csdrop.com/guides/doppler-phases) and [cspriceapi.com](https://cspriceapi.com/resources/cs2-doppler-phases-price-guide).
- Exception for **Butterfly Knife and Shadow Daggers**: Black Pearl = 617, Phase 2 = 618, Sapphire = 619. Ruby stays 415, and P1/P3/P4 stay 418/420/421. — [cspriceapi.com](https://cspriceapi.com/resources/cs2-doppler-phases-price-guide); [Clash stash, Butterfly Doppler](https://stash.clash.gg/skin/857/Butterfly-Knife-Doppler); [CSDROP Wiki](https://wiki.csdrop.com/guides/doppler-phases)
- **Talon Knife (unresolved):** one source says Talon Phases 1–4 use 852–855. Another says Talon Ruby, Sapphire and Black Pearl use the standard 415–417. One CSBoard FAQ gives "pattern ranges" (e.g. 853–917 = Sapphire), but its other ranges are internally impossible, so it is judged unreliable. — [search summary across cspriceapi / CSDROP / CSBoard](https://csboard.com/en/blog/butterfly-knife-doppler-guide-phases-prices-trading-on-csboard-2026)
- **Gamma Doppler, knives:** Emerald 568, Phase 1 569, Phase 2 570, Phase 3 571, Phase 4 572. **Glock-18 Gamma Doppler:** Emerald 1119, Phase 1–4 = 1120–1123. — [cspriceapi.com](https://cspriceapi.com/resources/cs2-doppler-phases-price-guide); matched by [CSDROP Wiki](https://wiki.csdrop.com/guides/doppler-phases)
- The phase is fixed when the item is created and can be checked from its catalog or paint index. Guides advise identifying the phase by **paint index, not colour**. — [CSDROP Wiki](https://wiki.csdrop.com/guides/doppler-phases)
- **Contradiction:** one price-tracker summary says "the pattern seed fixes the phase". — [steamanalyst.com Emerald pages (snippet)](https://www.steamanalyst.com/skin/m9-bayonet-gamma-doppler-emerald). This conflicts with the index tables above and with guides that say plainly "Does the paint seed change the phase? No." — [steamanalyst Doppler guide](https://www.steamanalyst.com/guides/doppler-phases) / [csgo-guides.com](https://csgo-guides.com/patterns/doppler)

**Items and wears**
- **Gamma Doppler knives:** Bayonet, M9 Bayonet, Karambit, Flip, Gut, Bowie, Falchion, Shadow Daggers, Huntsman, Butterfly. The **Glock-18** is the only gun with the finish, including an Emerald variant. — [CSDROP Wiki](https://wiki.csdrop.com/guides/doppler-phases); [wiki.cs.money Glock Emerald](https://wiki.cs.money/weapons/glock-18/gamma-doppler-emerald); [csgoskins.gg Glock-18 Gamma Doppler](https://csgoskins.gg/items/glock-18-gamma-doppler)
- **Float range:** knife Doppler and Gamma Doppler run 0.00–0.08 (FN/MW only). The Glock-18 Gamma Doppler runs 0.00–0.50. — [CSDROP / cspriceapi summary](https://wiki.csdrop.com/guides/doppler-phases). One source gives 0.00–0.07 for Doppler, which conflicts. — [blog.cs2.ad](https://blog.cs2.ad/doppler-phases/)
- **Colours:** P1 is dark purple/pink/black, P2 pink/purple, P3 blue/green/black, P4 blue. Ruby is all red, Sapphire all blue, Black Pearl dark purple or black with a rainbow sheen. Descriptions differ between sites, and cs2.ad contradicts itself on P4. — [profilerr.net](https://profilerr.net/doppler-phases-in-cs2/); [blog.cs2.ad](https://blog.cs2.ad/doppler-phases/); [winio.ai](https://winio.ai/blog/articles/cs2-bfk-doppler-guide)
- **Gamma Doppler colours:** Emerald is solid green across the blade. P2 is the closest to Emerald (bright green and turquoise). P1, P3 and P4 mix turquoise, blue, purple and green. — [csgo-guides.com Gamma Doppler](https://csgo-guides.com/patterns/gamma-doppler); [csmarket.gg](https://csmarket.gg/blog/a-comprehensive-guide-to-gamma-doppler-phases-in-cs2/)

**Seed within a phase**
- **Phase 2:** "Max Pink" or "Pink Galaxy" means only pink on the blade, with no purple. "BTA Pink" means mostly pink on the play side. — [Steam guide, M9 Doppler P2](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3323322399); [Steam guide, Butterfly P2](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3260663518)
- An example tier list for the M9 Bayonet P2 Pink Galaxy includes seeds 15, 35, 159, 200, 239, 261, 350, 355, 417, 474 and others. It is specific to the M9 and comes from a community Steam guide. — [Steam guide M9 P2](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3323322399)
- In the Butterfly P2 guide, patterns with blue spots get their own category, separate from the tier list. — [Steam guide Butterfly P2](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3260663518)
- **Phase 4:** very high blue coverage is called "Max Blue", "BTA Blue" or "Fake Sapphire" and sells at a premium. — [search summary: skinport / steamanalyst / csgo-guides](https://skinport.com/blog/cs2-doppler-phases-guide)
- One guide claims a Max Pink P2 sells at a **30–60% premium over an average P2**. Another says P2 carries a **21–53% premium over P4** on high-tier knives (M9, Karambit, Butterfly). Neither cites individual sales. — [csgo-guides.com](https://csgo-guides.com/patterns/doppler) / [cs2floatchecker.com](https://cs2floatchecker.com/blog/doppler-phases-guide). The snippets did not show which figure came from which page.
- Top P2 seeds differ by knife model. Lists that reuse the same numbers across knives are flagged as wrong, so seeds should be checked in a pattern viewer for that specific model. — [skin.land Bayonet P2](https://skin.land/blog/bayonet-doppler-phase-2-patterns-guide/)
- **Black Pearl:** a long-running community "Black Pearl Database" Steam guide exists. I could not read its content, including whether coverage drives a premium. — [Steam guide 793652529](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=793652529)

**Who rates them:** community Steam guides (seed tiers for each knife), pattern.wiki and Skinflow pattern checkers, and marketplace blogs (Skinport, skin.land, steamanalyst, csgo-guides, CSDROP). I found no official or neutral authority. — sources above; [pattern.wiki](https://pattern.wiki/skin/aq_oiled/weapon_knife_karambit/)

### Inferences
- For the calculator, model each phase as a **separate item** keyed by `(knife, paint_index)`. Treat the seed as a second-level modifier that applies only to the P2 and P4 sub-patterns, and to Gamma Doppler P2 or P4 "green" coverage where a source documents it.
- The Butterfly and Shadow Daggers indexes (617–619), plus the possible Talon indexes (852–855), mean a hard-coded 415–421 map will mislabel some knives.

### Gaps
- I could not verify the Talon Doppler indexes. Other newer knives may also have their own indexes (Navaja, Stiletto, Ursus, Skeleton, etc.). The full list of knives with Doppler is unconfirmed here.
- I found no documented seed premium for Gamma Doppler P1–P4 (e.g. "max green P2"), or for Ruby, Sapphire or Emerald coverage. I also could not read Black Pearl coverage criteria.
- No source gave the case or collection origin of the Glock-18 Gamma Doppler.
- I could not reach a neutral rating body. All tier lists are community or marketplace content.

## 2. How much more do Ruby, Sapphire, Black Pearl and Emerald cost than the regular phases?

### Takeaway
On the Karambit, tracker prices put **Ruby and Black Pearl at roughly 3–5× Phase 2** in FN. For Gamma Doppler, Emerald sits at **roughly 4–6× the numbered phases** on top knives (M9, Butterfly) but only about 2× on cheap knives (Shadow Daggers). **I found no individual dated sale records.** Every figure is a tracker or listing snapshot.

### Cited Findings
- **Karambit Doppler FN (tracker, date unknown):** Phase 2 about $1,698 on Steam Analyst (another page shows about $1,978). Ruby about $7,418. Black Pearl about $8,881, cheapest phase P3 about $1,181. Another tracker shows Black Pearl at about $8,212. — [steamanalyst Karambit Doppler](https://www.steamanalyst.com/skin/karambit-doppler); [steamanalyst Ruby FN](https://www.steamanalyst.com/skin/karambit-doppler-factory-new-ruby)
- **Karambit Ruby on steamdb.com:** listed "from $6,980" (StatTrak floor, a listing). — [steamdb.com Karambit Ruby](https://steamdb.com/en/skins/cs2/knives/karambit-doppler-ruby)
- **Karambit Ruby 2026 price-history range:** $6,716.44–$9,095.16, with the high on **2026-02-28** (tracker history, not a single sale). — [steamanalyst Karambit Ruby](https://www.steamanalyst.com/skin/karambit-doppler-factory-new-ruby)
- One guide orders live FN prices as **Black Pearl > Ruby > Sapphire > P2 > P4 > P1 > P3**. — [steamanalyst Doppler guide](https://www.steamanalyst.com/guides/doppler-phases)
- **Conflicting higher claims:** some sites say Karambit Ruby sells for "$12,000–20,000" or "$15,000–25,000+". These contradict the tracker data and may be pre-October-2025-crash figures or unsourced estimates. — [skinswap blog 2026](https://skinswap.com/blog/en/cs2/karambit-doppler-phases-ranked-a-complete-buying-guide-for-2026/); [csboard Karambit 2026](https://csboard.com/en/blog/cs2-karambit-trading-guide-2026-en). The snippets did not show which site gave which figure.
- **Gamma Doppler Emerald FN (tracker, date unknown):** M9 Bayonet Emerald about $5,740 against P1–P4 at about $974–$1,371. Butterfly Emerald about $7,217 against P1–P4 at about $1,627–$2,069. Shadow Daggers Emerald about $181 (another page shows a $221 average) against P1–P4 at about $91–93. — [steamanalyst M9 Emerald](https://www.steamanalyst.com/skin/m9-bayonet-gamma-doppler-emerald); [steamanalyst Butterfly GD](https://www.steamanalyst.com/skin/butterfly-knife-gamma-doppler); [steamanalyst Shadow Daggers Emerald](https://www.steamanalyst.com/skin/shadow-daggers-gamma-doppler/factory-new/emerald); [cs2.sh Shadow Daggers Emerald](https://cs2.sh/item/shadow-daggers-gamma-doppler-emerald)
- **Generic multipliers (guides, unsourced):** Emerald "9×+ market price" and P2 at 1.1–1.5× in one guide. Emerald 4.4–4.6× Phase 4 and P2 1.3–1.5× in another. — [csgo-guides.com Gamma Doppler](https://csgo-guides.com/patterns/gamma-doppler) / [cspriceapi.com](https://cspriceapi.com/resources/cs2-doppler-phases-price-guide)
- **Rarity:** one source estimates each gem at about 1–2% of Doppler drops and says Valve has not confirmed the rates. — [summary incl. goldfieldstvet.edu.za page, a dubious domain](https://goldfieldstvet.edu.za/pags/ruby_sapphire_black_pearl_cs2.html)
- One steamdb.com price snapshot is from **June 2026**. — [steamdb.com Doppler guide](https://steamdb.com/en/articles/cs2-doppler-phases-guide)

### Inferences
- The gem multiplier grows with the base knife's price: about 2× on Shadow Daggers and about 4–6× on M9, Butterfly and Karambit. A single fixed multiplier would misprice cheap knives.
- Sapphire's exact Karambit price was not captured. Its rank in the ordering suggests it sits between Ruby and P2.

### Gaps
- **No individual sale records with date and link** (CSFloat, Buff or Steam sale history) were reachable. These are all listing or tracker aggregates.
- No price data for Sapphire, Glock-18 Emerald, or Black Pearl on other knives.
- I do not know how the October 2025 crash changed the gem multipliers over time.

## 3. Other finishes: does the seed matter for Tiger Tooth, Damascus Steel, Lore, Autotronic, Bright Water, Freehand, Night and Ultraviolet?

### Takeaway
I found evidence of seed-dependent looks for **Tiger Tooth**, **Damascus Steel** and **Freehand**, but **no documented premium seed tiers** for any of them. Autotronic is listed with **no pattern variants**. For Lore, Night, Ultraviolet and Bright Water I found nothing beyond availability, and one source says Bright Water knives look much alike.

### Cited Findings
- **Tiger Tooth:** csgoskins.gg lists "Pattern Variants: Yes" for the Talon, Stiletto, Survival, Paracord and Skeleton versions. — [csgoskins.gg Paracord TT](https://csgoskins.gg/items/paracord-knife-tiger-tooth); [Talon TT](https://csgoskins.gg/items/talon-knife-tiger-tooth). CS.MONEY describes how, on the Karambit, some seeds give wide, frequent stripes while others leave a bright orange base with only thin lines. — [cs.money shapeshifting patterns (CS:GO-era)](https://cs.money/blog/cs-go-skins/unique-csgo-shapeshifting-patterns/). No price premium is documented.
- **Damascus Steel:** its look depends on the pattern index ([csgoskins.gg Gut Damascus](https://csgoskins.gg/items/gut-knife-damascus-steel)), and it "can get much brighter if the distance between the lines becomes larger" ([cs.money](https://cs.money/blog/cs-go-skins/unique-csgo-shapeshifting-patterns/)). No premium is documented, and I found no "red/black" Damascus premium either.
- **Autotronic:** the Karambit Autotronic page lists **"Pattern Variants: No"**. The finish is red/grey, Gunsmith style, about $1,413 FN and about $996 MW (snapshot, date unknown). — [csgoskins.gg Karambit Autotronic](https://csgoskins.gg/items/karambit-autotronic). No "blue gem"-style tiers exist. The Karambit seed tiers that do exist (e.g. 387) belong to Case Hardened. — [csgoskins.gg blog](https://csgoskins.gg/blog/karambit-case-hardened-blue-gem-seed-patterns)
- **Lore:** I found no seed or premium information. One Bayonet listing shows Lore at $175, Autotronic $210 and Damascus $136. These figures look implausibly low for FN and may be low-wear or stale. — [stash.clash.gg / hltv summary](https://stash.clash.gg/weapon/Flip-Knife)
- **Freehand:** a Chroma 3 finish (2016) on Bayonet, Flip, Gut, Karambit and M9. The blue brushwork lands differently on each copy, and buyers prefer a well-centred, prominent swirl on the front of the blade. This is a qualitative preference, not a tier list. — [steamanalyst Freehand guide 2026](https://www.steamanalyst.com/guides/freehand); Skinflow has seed pages for each knife ([Butterfly Freehand](https://skinflow.gg/pattern-checker/butterfly-knife/freehand))
- **Bright Water:** pattern.wiki has seed pages (texture `hy_ocean_knife_90`). — [pattern.wiki Butterfly](https://pattern.wiki/skin/hy_ocean_knife_90/weapon_knife_butterfly/). One guide says most Bright Water knives look alike, so pattern premiums are unlikely. Sources also disagree on which knives carry the finish. — [critfeed](https://critfeed.com/best-bright-water-knife-glove-combos-cs2/) / [steamanalyst paint-seed guide](https://www.steamanalyst.com/guides/paint-seed-explained)
- **Night and Ultraviolet:** listed as knife finishes only. I found no pattern or premium information. — [csgoskins.gg Flip Knife list](https://csgoskins.gg/weapons/flip-knife)
- A community "Top 3 Patterns for Every Knife in CS2" Steam guide exists, but I could not read it. — [Steam guide 3538425089](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3538425089)

### Inferences
- For the calculator, treat **Autotronic, Lore, Night, Ultraviolet and Bright Water as having no seed premium**, labelled "none documented". Treat Tiger Tooth, Damascus and Freehand as "cosmetic variation, no established premium". Slaughter, Case Hardened, Fade and Marble Fade are covered by other researchers.

### Gaps
- I could not confirm whether a Lore "gold or full-artwork" seed premium exists anywhere.
- No sale data shows any premium for Tiger Tooth, Damascus or Freehand seeds.

## 4. Can you get Doppler phases from a trade-up? Current rules, and how phase and seed are chosen

### Takeaway
Yes. Since the CS2 update of **2025-10-22/23** (sources differ on the date, probably because of time zones), a Trade Up Contract accepts **5 Covert items** and returns **1 knife or pair of gloves** from the collection of one of the inputs. StatTrak inputs return a StatTrak knife only. Any Doppler phase or gem the case can drop, including Black Pearl, is a possible result. **Valve does not publish how the finish, phase or seed is weighted.** I found no 2026 rule change.

### Cited Findings
- Hotspawn reproduces the patch notes: "Extended functionality of the 'Trade Up Contract' to allow exchanging 5 items of Covert quality." StatTrak inputs yield a StatTrak knife. Non-StatTrak inputs yield a knife or gloves from the collection of one of the input items. Dated **October 23, 2025**. — [Hotspawn](https://www.hotspawn.com/counter-strike/news/new-cs2-update-adds-retakes-and-ability-to-trade-up-to-knife-and-gloves); csdb.gg says "Since October 22, 2025". — [csdb.gg](https://csdb.gg/guides/cs2-knife-trade-ups/)
- StatTrak and non-StatTrak inputs cannot be mixed. StatTrak gloves do not exist, so StatTrak inputs cannot produce gloves. — [thespike.gg](https://www.thespike.gg/counter-strike-2/beginner-guides/trade-ups-cs2-guide); [skin.club guide 2026](https://community.skin.club/en/articles/trade-up-contracts-guide-cs2)
- You can narrow the pool by choosing input collections, but not pick the exact knife or finish. — [csdb.gg](https://csdb.gg/guides/cs2-knife-trade-ups/); [refrag.gg](https://refrag.gg/blog/cs2-skins-update-trade-ups/)
- **Odds model (disputed):** skin.club says each input carries an equal share, 20% in a 5-Covert contract, based on community testing, and Valve does not publish the odds. steamdb.com gives a formula weighted by inputs per collection and that collection's next-tier item count. White Market, a seller, says knife types are roughly equally weighted at about 16–20% per type. — [skin.club](https://community.skin.club/en/articles/trade-up-contracts-guide-cs2); [steamdb.com trade-up guide](https://steamdb.com/en/articles/cs2-trade-up-contract-guide); [white.market](https://white.market/blog/guides/how-to-get-a-karambit-in-cs2-via-trade-up-complete-2026-guide)
- The output float is said to come from the average float of the inputs. The exact formula was not shown in the snippets. — [same search summary; steamdb.com trade-up guide](https://steamdb.com/en/articles/cs2-trade-up-contract-guide)
- Doppler, all phases including Black Pearl, is listed among possible Spectrum-case Butterfly results. — [white.market Butterfly guide](https://white.market/blog/guides/how-to-get-a-butterfly-knife-in-cs2-via-trade-up-complete-2026-guide)
- **Market impact:** about $1.75B was reportedly wiped off within hours, about 25% in a day. Later recovery projections are speculative. — [esports.gg](https://esports.gg/news/counter-strike-2/knives-trade-up-contracts-cs2-update); [club386](https://www.club386.com/?p=121563)

### Inferences
- Phase and gem are probably drawn as separate finishes (paint indexes) inside the case's knife pool, and the seed is probably random over 0–1000 as in case drops. Neither is confirmed by Valve or by any source I could read. The calculator should label phase and gem odds as "unknown / assumed as case-drop distribution".
- Because the output wear comes from the input float average and Doppler is capped at 0.08, the clamping and remapping of the output float matters for Doppler outcomes. This is unverified.

### Gaps
- I could not read Valve's original patch note at counter-strike.net (blocked).
- I found no source on whether Doppler phases or gems are equally weighted in trade-up outputs, or on how often gems come from trade-ups.
- No confirmation that the rules were unchanged through October 2026. I simply found no reports of changes.
