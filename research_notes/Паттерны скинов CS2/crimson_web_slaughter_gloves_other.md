Research date: 2026-10-19. METHOD NOTE: the session's egress proxy blocked every direct page fetch (steamdb.com, wiki.cs.money, pricempire.com, csfloat.com, reddit, pattern.wiki, profilerr.net, hltv.org all returned "CONNECT tunnel failed, 403"). ## Crimson Web: which items, wear, how the seed places webs, centered/double/triple webs

### Takeaway
Crimson Web is the main "web count/placement" finish. The seed (0–1000) sets where the web texture sits. Buyers pay for one well-centered web on the visible (playside) face, or for triple webs on large blades (the M9 Bayonet especially). Wear matters twice: the black webs are the first thing to fade, so a premium seed in mid/high Field-Tested is barely worth more than an ordinary copy. Sources agree that the Karambit has no triple webs. They disagree on exact tier-1 seed lists.

### Cited Findings
**Items and wear range**
- Crimson Web exists on many knives (Karambit, M9 Bayonet, Butterfly, Flip, Nomad, Skeleton, Kukri, Survival, Paracord, Stiletto, Falchion, Bowie, Classic, etc.) and on Specialist Gloves. Knife float range is 0.06–0.80, so all five exteriors exist, and StatTrak versions exist — [csgoskins.gg Nomad Crimson Web](https://csgoskins.gg/items/nomad-knife-crimson-web), [prosettings Survival Knife CW](https://prosettings.net/items/survival-knife-crimson-web/), [HLTV Butterfly CW](https://www.hltv.org/skins/knife/butterfly-knife/crimson-web)
- The finish also exists on the Desert Eagle (Hotspawn lists "Crimson Web" among the Deagle's pattern-based skins) — [Hotspawn: All pattern-based CS2 skins](https://www.hotspawn.com/counter-strike/news/all-pattern-based-cs2-skins)
- Wear is destructive on this finish. "The black webs are the first thing to fade as the float climbs… by the middle of Field-Tested the webs on many models are grey smudges." The premium for a triple web "only holds on clean copies; a triple web at 0.30 float is a triple web nobody can see, and the market prices it that way." — [P2Trades: Crimson Web patterns, "Why Float Matters Twice"](https://www.p2trades.com/guides/crimson-web-patterns)
- Profilerr: high wear "will destroy the pattern anyway", so the seed matters less on heavily worn units — [Profilerr Karambit CW guide](https://profilerr.net/karambit-crimson-web-guide/)

**Mechanics / what is valued**
- The best Karambit seeds have a web close to the center of the blade, where the owner sees it. A single centered web is usually preferred over several webs, because "too many details on a small surface" looks worse — [Profilerr Karambit CW](https://profilerr.net/karambit-crimson-web-guide/); "the more center the web is, the better" — [Pricempire Karambit CW guide](https://pricempire.com/guides/karambit-crimson-web-guide)
- SteamAnalyst's centering advice: Karambit web near the middle of the curved blade; M9 web on the upper, playside section — [SteamAnalyst Crimson Web Pattern Guide (2026)](https://www.steamanalyst.com/guides/crimson-web-pattern)

**Karambit**
- 41 seeds are rated Tier 1 (centered single webs). Example Tier 1 seeds that appear in several lists: 390, 515, 964 — [Pricempire](https://pricempire.com/guides/karambit-crimson-web-guide); [Profilerr](https://profilerr.net/karambit-crimson-web-guide/); [cs2.io Crimson Web seeds](https://cs2.io/en/skins/patterns/crimson-web)
- Triple webs do not exist on the Karambit: "blade is too small for triple webs, so none exist among the 1000 seeds". Double webs exist but add no premium — [cs2.io](https://cs2.io/en/skins/patterns/crimson-web); Pricempire also says Karambit triple webs are "non-existent" — [Pricempire](https://pricempire.com/guides/karambit-crimson-web-guide)
- One guide counts only 12 Karambit double webs, which it does not sort into tiers — [CS-Resource Karambit CW guide](https://cs-resource.com/en/karambit-crimson-web-pattern-guide/)
- A search summary of the SteamDB Crimson Web page showed both "no triple webs in 1000 seeds" and "~45 of 1000 seeds have three webs". The ~45 figure matches Pricempire's M9 Bayonet count (below), so the SteamDB page most likely covers several knives and the summary mixed them up. This is unverified because the page could not be opened — [SteamDB Crimson Web patterns](https://steamdb.com/en/skins/cs2/patterns/crimson-web)
- The Tier 1 lists in the CS-Resource table and the BitSkins guide do not fully match — [CS-Resource](https://cs-resource.com/en/karambit-crimson-web-pattern-guide/), [BitSkins blog](https://bitskins.com/blog/karambit-crimson-web-best-web-pattern-guide/)

**M9 Bayonet**
- Pricempire counts about 45 triple-web seeds out of 1000 and 418 single-web seeds. It calls triple webs the most desirable, and names seed 718 as the best-aligned triple web "by most" — [Pricempire M9 CW guide](https://pricempire.com/guides/m9-bayonet-crimson-web-guide)
- Pricempire's Tier 1 triple-web list: 72, 13, 375, 646, 944, 114, 513, 857, 718, 297. Skin.land's list matches but adds 423 and 469 — [Pricempire](https://pricempire.com/guides/m9-bayonet-crimson-web-guide); [Skin.land M9 CW guide](https://skin.land/blog/the-best-m9-bayonet-crimson-web-patterns-guide/)
- Profilerr calls #706 "one of the most famous M9 Bayonet triple hub patterns". Its guide says triple webs "can cost multiple times more", and gives an M9 CW top range of about $480–$16,500 (a general range, not a dated sale) — [Profilerr M9 CW guide](https://profilerr.net/m9-bayonet-crimson-web-guide/)
- Disagreement: Profilerr's flagship M9 seed is #706, and Pricempire's is #718.

**Butterfly**

Its list does not match other Butterfly lists — [Skin.land Butterfly CW guide](https://skin.land/blog/the-butterfly-knife-crimson-web-pattern-guide/); [Steam guide: Butterfly CW Pattern Guide](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3498320672)

**Prices (snapshots; mostly listings, not sales)**

StatTrak FN listings run from $10,889 to $50,000 asking, with a tracker market average of about $25,486 and a lowest CSFloat listing of $25,000. These are listings and averages, not completed sales. The search summary gave no exact date (around Oct 2026) — [SteamAnalyst Karambit CW](https://www.steamanalyst.com/skin/karambit-crimson-web), [SteamAnalyst ST FN](https://www.steamanalyst.com/skin/karambit-crimson-web/stattrak-factory-new), [lis-skins listing](https://lis-skins.com/market/csgo/%E2%98%85-karambit-crimson-web-factory-new/)
- SteamAnalyst's guide says centered FN Karambit CWs "can sell for $10,000–$50,000+", but its own price table shows the Karambit at $571 (BS) to $7,304 (FN). The claim is not tied to any cited sale and looks inconsistent with the table — [SteamAnalyst CW guide](https://www.steamanalyst.com/guides/crimson-web-pattern)
- M9 CW FN: from about $3,976 to $4,129, with lowest Skinport offers around $4,077 (listings) — [csgoskins.gg M9 CW](https://csgoskins.gg/items/m9-bayonet-crimson-web), [SteamAnalyst M9 CW](https://www.steamanalyst.com/skin/m9-bayonet-crimson-web)
- Butterfly CW: Factory New $3,900, Minimal Wear $965, Field-Tested $608 (snapshot; the FN/FT gap shows the wear premium) — [HLTV Butterfly CW](https://www.hltv.org/skins/knife/butterfly-knife/crimson-web)

### Inferences
- For a calculator, the most defensible "premium" rule is a centered single web for small blades (Karambit) and a triple web for large blades (M9, Bayonet, Butterfly), applied only from FN to low MW. Beyond about mid-FT, apply no pattern premium. This follows the P2Trades and Profilerr logic.
- Exact Tier 1 lists vary by site, so the site should cite one source per knife (e.g., Pricempire), or show "listed by X / Y".

### Gaps
- No verified, dated, linkable completed sale of a specific premium Crimson Web seed (e.g., M9 #718 or #706, a Karambit Tier 1) was found. Search returned only listings, ranges and trackers' aggregate medians.
- Could not open SteamDB or wiki.cs.money to settle the triple-web contradiction or see their full seed tables.
- Per-seed web placement for the Desert Eagle Crimson Web: no source found.

## Slaughter: named shapes, seeds, wears

### Takeaway
Slaughter is a Covert knife (and Hand Wraps) finish with a 0.00/0.01–0.26 float cap, so only FN, MW and FT exist. The seed decides which abstract shapes form on the playside. The community names them Diamond, Heart, Angel, Phoenix, Zebra, Dogbone, Kitty, Butterfly, Clown, Face and others. Every source puts Diamond (and Diamond+Heart) at the top. Mid/low tiers differ by source. Seeds are knife-specific and vary between guides.

### Cited Findings
- Slaughter is Covert. Float runs 0.00–0.26 (one page says 0.01–0.26), so it exists only in FN/MW/FT — [wiki.cs.money Slaughter pages, e.g. Classic Knife](https://wiki.cs.money/weapons/classic-knife/slaughter); [SteamAnalyst Flip Slaughter](https://www.steamanalyst.com/skin/flip-knife-slaughter/field-tested); [CS-Resource Slaughter guide](https://cs-resource.com/en/slaughter-guide/)
- CS.MONEY Wiki: "Pattern variations with shapes resembling a diamond (or a diamond and a heart) are considered to be the rarest and the most expensive ones". Heart-only comes next. "Dogbone and angel shapes are less rare and considered to be the most common ones." — [wiki.cs.money Karambit Slaughter](https://wiki.cs.money/weapons/karambit/slaughter)
- Tier lists disagree:
  - Profilerr: top = Diamond, Heart; mid = Angel, Phoenix, Kitty; low = Dogbone, Zebra, Butterfly, Clown — [Profilerr Slaughter guide](https://profilerr.net/cs2-slaughter-patterns-guide/)
  - Old Steam guide (Vloshko): Common = Zebra, Face, White Diamond; Uncommon = Phoenix, Angel; Rare = Heart, Diamond — [Steam guide: Slaughter Patterns](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=708827104)
  - SteamAnalyst: Diamond and Angel = premium; Phoenix and Zebra = standard. Phoenix still "commands premiums when clearly visible and well-centered" — [SteamAnalyst Slaughter guide](https://www.steamanalyst.com/guides/slaughter-pattern)
  - Conflict: CS.MONEY rates Angel as common, while SteamAnalyst rates it premium.
- Phoenix vs Angel: in Phoenix "only the outer line of the pattern is visible", while in Angel the shape fills the whole surface — [CS-Resource](https://cs-resource.com/en/slaughter-guide/). Profilerr says Phoenix is seen as a "reversed" Angel — [Profilerr](https://profilerr.net/cs2-slaughter-patterns-guide/)
- On larger blades (the Bayonet), Diamond/Heart split into Tier 1 (both shapes centered) and Tier 2 (slightly off-center). On the Falchion, the diamond appears rotated 90° — [Profilerr (DE)](https://profilerr.net/de/slaughter-knife-pattern-guide-fuer-cs2-spieler/), [Profilerr (PL)](https://profilerr.net/pl/wzory-slaughter-cs2/)
- Example seeds (reported; lists conflict):
  - Bayonet Diamond/Heart Tier 1 includes 37, 120, 290, 335, 402, 666, 693, 727, 881, and Tier 2 includes 7 and 9. Double-diamond seeds include 90, 124 and 652, and #165 is named Tier 1 double diamond. These come from guides returned in one search ([Profilerr](https://profilerr.net/cs2-slaughter-patterns-guide/), [Steam guide: Top 3 patterns for every knife](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3538425089)); the summary did not make clear which page lists which seeds.
  - Karambit centered Diamond Tier 1: 349, 355, 948, 229 per one guide. Another lists 997 and 986. Karambit Heart Tier 1: 466, 585, 655, 689, 912, 973, with 147 named "popular" elsewhere — [Skin.land Karambit Slaughter guide](https://skin.land/blog/full-karambit-slaughter-patterns-guide/), [Steam guide: Slaughter Diamond Pattern](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2566200984)
  - M9 Bayonet Heart: 55 is named a popular seed (same search set)
- Visibility: SteamAnalyst's pattern checker says recognition "is subjective and depends on how clearly defined and centered the shape appears" — [SteamAnalyst Pattern Checker](https://www.steamanalyst.com/tools/pattern-checker)
- Prices: SteamAnalyst's guide gives a "Full Centered Diamond" FN Karambit at about $600–$1,200+ premium. The guide was about 255 days old at search time, and its basis is unknown. The lowest CSFloat Karambit Slaughter listings were about $968.69 (FN) and $815.87 (MW) for ordinary copies, around Oct 2026 — [SteamAnalyst Slaughter guide](https://www.steamanalyst.com/guides/slaughter-pattern), [SteamAnalyst Karambit Slaughter FN](https://www.steamanalyst.com/skin/karambit-slaughter/factory-new)

### Inferences
- Because wear is capped at FT 0.26, wear destroys Slaughter shapes far less than it does Crimson Web. Premium depends mainly on the shape and its centering, not on the float tier. No source states this directly; it follows from the float cap, and SteamAnalyst ties visibility to centering, not float.
- The low Karambit Slaughter floor (~$969 FN) fits the post-Oct-2025 knife price crash (see trade-up section).

### Gaps
- No dated completed sale of a named Slaughter diamond/heart seed was found.
- Could not open wiki.cs.money to get its own per-knife seed table.
- Hand Wraps | Slaughter: no seed guide or premium documentation found.

## Gloves: documented seed premiums

### Takeaway
Seed premiums on gloves are documented only for specific finishes with discrete motifs or color zones: Specialist Gloves Crimson Web (centered webs), Specialist Blackbook (symbols on both hands, e.g. "Double Dicky" #30), Specialist Cloud Chaser (double dragon, e.g. #551), Hand Wraps Overprint ("Arrow" tiers), Sport Gloves Ultra Violent (max purple/cyan), Sport Gloves Big Game (orange coverage) and Hydra Gloves Case Hardened (blue). For Sport Gloves Pandora's Box, one guide says there is "not really a market" for special patterns. No seed-premium guides were found for Sport Gloves Vice, Hedge Maze, Specialist Fade, Driver Gloves, or Hand Wraps Slaughter.

### Cited Findings
- Specialist Gloves | Crimson Web: the most valuable seeds have a web center on the central part of both gloves. Tiering is based on the right glove (visible when holding a knife). Tier 1 example #914, Tier 2 #916. Another guide gives #323 Tier 1, #208 Tier 2, #233 Tier 3. Most seeds put webs off-model or clip them at the edge — [Steam guide: Specialist Gloves "Crimson Web" Pattern Guide & Tierlist](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2818147579), [CSGUI.DE Specialist CW guide](https://csgui.de/best-specialist-gloves-crimson-web-patterns-guide/), [wiki.cs.money Specialist CW](https://wiki.cs.money/gloves/specialist-gloves-crimson-web)
- pattern.wiki publishes per-seed texture offsets for Specialist CW (e.g., seed 0: X 0.416 / Y 0.092), with seeds 0–1000 — [pattern.wiki Specialist Gloves CW](https://pattern.wiki/skin/specialist_webs_red/specialist_gloves/)
- Specialist Gloves | Blackbook: symbol seeds such as "Double Dicky" #30 and "Double DED". A normal pair costs about $170 (BS) to $1,500+ (FN) — [Hotspawn Blackbook guide](https://www.hotspawn.com/counter-strike/news/specialist-gloves-blackbook-patterns-guide), [lis-skins Blackbook](https://lis-skins.com/blog/best-specialist-gloves-blackbook-patterns/)
- Specialist Gloves | Cloud Chaser: double-dragon seeds ranked by centering, with #551 cited as top. Buyers often take FT/WW and hunt for the seed instead — [Hotspawn Cloud Chaser](https://www.hotspawn.com/counter-strike/news/specialist-gloves-cloud-chaser-patterns-guide), [Skin.land Cloud Chaser](https://skin.land/blog/the-best-specialist-gloves-cloud-chaser-patterns/)
- Hand Wraps | Overprint: Tier 1 "Arrow" seeds cover the knuckle and wrist tape fully with arrows — [csgoskins.gg Overprint guide](https://csgoskins.gg/blog/hand-wraps-overprint-guide-all-rare-seed-patterns)
- Sport Gloves | Ultra Violent: Tier 1/2 max-purple and max-cyan seeds. Max purple is pitched as a cheaper Vice alternative — [Hotspawn Ultra Violent](https://www.hotspawn.com/counter-strike/news/sport-gloves-ultra-violent-patterns-guide)
- Sport Gloves | Big Game (orange coverage) and Hydra Gloves | Case Hardened (blue) have seed guides — [lis-skins Big Game](https://lis-skins.com/blog/the-best-sport-gloves-big-game-patterns-guide/), [Skin.land Hydra CH](https://skin.land/blog/all-best-hydra-gloves-case-hardened-patterns/)
- Pandora's Box: the creatures are randomized, but "there's not really a market for special Pandora's Box patterns". One glove guide says model, finish and float explain "almost the entire price" — [ProSettings: Best CS2 Gloves](https://prosettings.net/guides/best-cs2-gloves/)
- Hedge Maze: FT about $3,800 per ProSettings (undated snapshot). The CSMarketCap FN lowest listing was $2,206.42 on 2026-07-29. SteamAnalyst shows a $37,119.58 figure for Hedge Maze, of unclear basis — [ProSettings](https://prosettings.net/guides/best-cs2-gloves/), [CSMarketCap Hedge Maze FN](https://csmarketcap.com/item/sport-gloves-hedge-maze/factory-new), [SteamAnalyst gloves](https://www.steamanalyst.com/browse/gloves)

### Inferences
- For a calculator, glove pattern flags are worth showing for Specialist CW, Blackbook, Cloud Chaser, Overprint, Ultra Violent, Big Game and Hydra CH. Pandora's Box, Vice, Hedge Maze, Fade and Driver Gloves should be marked "no documented seed premium (as of Oct 2026)".

## Other guns/knives with documented seed premiums

### Takeaway
Besides Case Hardened, Fade and Doppler (likely covered elsewhere), the clearest documented gun premium is the Glock-18 Fade fade percentage, which depends on the seed. Real CSFloat-based premiums there are modest (about +26% for 99–100%). Hotspawn's master list shows many Deagle and other finishes as "pattern-based", but no seed premiums were found for AK-47 Fire Serpent, Desert Eagle Blaze, AK Hydroponic, M4A1-S Printstream, AWP Wildfire or Butterfly Lore. Those should be marked undocumented.

### Cited Findings
- Glock-18 Fade: the fade % comes from the seed on an 80–100% scale (used by Skinport and CSGOSKINS.GG). Of 1,001 seeds, 62 reach 99%, 109 reach 98%, 261 reach 95% and 501 reach 90%. Seed #763 is consistently #1, followed by #575, #897 and #636 — [csgoskins.gg Glock Fade %](https://csgoskins.gg/blog/glock-18-fade-percentage-values-seed-patterns), [SteamAnalyst Glock Fade guide](https://www.steamanalyst.com/guides/glock-18-fade)

Another guide claims 20–50%+, and others claim 100–200% or 2–3x (unsourced). The terminology conflicts: Pricempire uses "Absolute Max" (4 patterns) and "100% Full Fade" (5), while others call 95–99% "Max Fade" — [SteamAnalyst Glock Fade](https://www.steamanalyst.com/guides/glock-18-fade), [Pricempire Glock Fade guide](https://pricempire.com/guides/glock-18-fade-guide), [cs2floatchecker rare patterns](https://cs2floatchecker.com/blog/cs2-rare-patterns-guide)

- Hotspawn's Deagle pattern-based list: Blaze, Bronze Deco, Cobalt Disruption, Crimson Web, Emerald Jörmungandr, Golden Koi, Heat Treated, Hypnotic, Meteorite, Midnight Storm. Printstream is not listed — [Hotspawn](https://www.hotspawn.com/counter-strike/news/all-pattern-based-cs2-skins)
- CS.MONEY says CS2 skins with pattern-dependent looks now exceed 100 — [CS.MONEY patterns guide](https://cs.money/blog/cs-go-skins/full-csgo-patterns-guide/)
- AK-47 Fire Serpent: no seed list was found. Its value is driven by rarity and wear (StatTrak MW is among the priciest AKs) — [cs2floatchecker](https://cs2floatchecker.com/blog/cs2-rare-patterns-guide)

## Trade-up: chance of landing a given seed

### Takeaway
Since Valve's Oct 22/23 2025 update, 5 Covert skins from one collection can be traded up into a knife or gloves from that collection's gold pool. StatTrak works for knives but not gloves. Sources show seeds run 0–1000 (1,001 values). No source was found that documents how the trade-up output seed is chosen. The community assumption is uniform random, giving about 1/1001 per exact seed.

### Cited Findings
- Oct 2025 update: 5 Covert → 1 knife/glove from the gold pool of an input's collection. StatTrak version included. StatTrak gloves are not available this way. The dates given are Oct 22 or 23, 2025 — [esports.gg](https://esports.gg/news/counter-strike-2/knives-trade-up-contracts-cs2-update), [Dust2.us](https://www.dust2.us/news/66919/retakes-and-a-new-way-to-get-knives-debut-in-october-22nd-counter-strike-2-update), [csdb.gg knife trade-ups](https://csdb.gg/guides/cs2-knife-trade-ups/), [skinbase.io](https://skinbase.io/blog/october-2025-cs2-trade-up-knives-gloves)
- Market impact: about $2B in value "evaporated within 48 hours" (per one guide), and SkinBid filed for insolvency in Nov 2025 (same guide; unverified elsewhere) — [esports.net market crash](https://www.esports.net/wiki/guides/cs2-knives-skin-market-crash/), [csdb.gg](https://csdb.gg/guides/cs2-knife-trade-ups/)
- Outcome odds: P(item) = (inputs from its collection / N inputs) × (1 / items in the next tier of that collection). The output float is deterministic (normalized-average formula) — [SteamDB trade-up guide](https://steamdb.com/en/articles/cs2-trade-up-contract-guide), [csdb.gg float guide](https://csdb.gg/guides/float-guide/)
- Seed range 0–1000 (1,001 seeds): [pattern.wiki](https://pattern.wiki/skin/specialist_webs_red/specialist_gloves/), [csgoskins.gg Glock Fade "1,001 seeds"](https://csgoskins.gg/blog/glock-18-fade-percentage-values-seed-patterns)

### Inferences
- If the seed is uniform over 1,001 values, then given that you got the target knife and finish: P(a specific seed) ≈ 0.1%. P(any Karambit CW Tier 1, 41 seeds) ≈ 4.1%. P(any M9 CW triple web, ~45 seeds) ≈ 4.5%. P(Glock Fade ≥99%, 62 seeds) ≈ 6.2%. Multiply by the chance of getting that exact knife and finish from the contract. This is an inference, not a documented Valve rule.
- For Crimson Web, the output float matters as much as the seed. Because the float is deterministic, a calculator can tell users in advance whether a premium seed would even be visible (FN/low MW).

### Gaps
- No primary or official source on how the trade-up output seed is generated (uniform random is assumed, not confirmed).
