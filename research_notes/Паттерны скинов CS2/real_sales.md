## Q1. Record-breaking deals: what actually sold, when, for how much

### Takeaway
Only a handful of pattern-driven deals have solid reporting. The biggest is the StatTrak FN AK-47 Case Hardened #661 (June 2024, "over $1M", exact price never disclosed). Before that came the StatTrak MW AK #661 with 4x Titan Holo Kato 2014 (April 2023, about $400k inside a $500k+ bundle) and the FT Karambit CH #387 (July 2023, about $169k, reported). The famous FN Karambit #387 "$1.5M" figure is a **rejected 2021 offer, not a sale**. For Doppler Ruby/Sapphire/Black Pearl/Emerald, Fire & Ice, Crimson Web, Slaughter and gloves, I found **no press-reported record sales**, only tracker or marketplace data.

## Q2. How trustworthy is each claim?

### Takeaway
The trust ladder runs: tracker-recorded marketplace sales (verifiable price, small samples) > middleman-announced private deals echoed by major outlets (completion credible, price only approximate) > single-outlet "reported" deals > listicle claims > offers and asking prices (not sales at all).

### Cited Findings
- AK #661 ST FN (2024): announced by two named, well-known middlemen. Every outlet reports only "over $1M", with the exact price "never officially disclosed". Brewminate advises treating it as "a reported private sale rather than a fully transparent marketplace record" — [esports.gg](https://esports.gg/news/counter-strike-2/cs2-skin-reportedly-sold-for-over-1-million); [Insider Gaming](https://insider-gaming.com/counter-strike-skin-sells-1-million/); [Brewminate](https://brewminate.com/most-expensive-csgo-and-cs2-skins-in-history/).
- AK #661 ST MW (2023): the $400k-of-$500k split rests on zipeL's claim. Most reports say the buyer was a Chinese collector, but one notes there is "no official information regarding the buyer" — [PC Gamer](https://www.pcgamer.com/counter-strike-skin-sells-for-dollar400k-probably-the-most-expensive-gun-in-videogame-history/); [Dexerto](https://www.dexerto.com/csgo/ak-47-csgo-skin-knife-sell-for-500000-as-counter-strike-skins-explode-in-price-2115567/).
- Karambit #387 FT ($169k): WIN.GG says it was "yet to be finalized" when reported. The price spread via Jake Lucky's social media — [WIN.GG](https://win.gg/news/the-noob-blue-gem-karambit-has-sold-for-169000/).
- Karambit #387 FN: the $1.5M figure is a rejected offer; "no knife has been publicly confirmed to sell for $1 million or more" — [GHOSTCAP](https://www.ghostcap.com/most-expensive-cs2-knife); [skins.cash list](https://skins.cash/blog/most-expensive-cs2-knives/).
- Aggregator conflicts: Escorenews says a "MW with four Titan holos sold for $160,000 in March 2023". Dexerto shows the March 2023 $160k sale was an AK **Wild Lotus**, not the CH #661, so the Escorenews line conflates two deals — [Escorenews](https://escorenews.com/en/news/59180-cs2-skin-ak-47-case-hardened-pattern-661-sells-for-record-breaking-price-over-1-mln-it-s-now-the-most-expensive-skin-in-history) vs [Dexerto](https://www.dexerto.com/csgo/csgo-ak-47-skin-sells-for-160000-in-one-of-the-biggest-skin-sales-ever-2085770/). Low-quality lists give about $400k for #661 and about $150k for #387, which contradicts the better sources — per search summary of [csdb](https://csdb.gg/guides/most-expensive-cs2-skins/) / [siege.gg](https://siege.gg/news/top-10-most-expensive-cs2-skins-ever-sold-prices-dates-why-theyre-so-valuable).
- Tracker-recorded prices (cs2pattern-prices.com) are completed CSFloat cash transactions, according to the site. They have not been independently verified, and some top prices are sticker-driven (M4A1-S #441) — [cs2pattern-prices.com](https://cs2pattern-prices.com/weapons/m4a1-s/fade/99/441).
- Label fraud is a known risk: a seller calling an item "Fire & Ice" does not guarantee it is one, and many "F&I" listings are backside or Tier 2 — [eatingwithkirby guide](https://eatingwithkirby.com/news/marble_fade_guide_cs2.html); [shadowpay](https://shadowpay.com/blog/skins/cs2-karambit-marble-fade-fire-ice-patterns-rarity-value/).

### Inferences
- In site copy, item 1 is safe to state as "reportedly sold for over $1M (June 2024)". Do not state a fixed number such as "$1.5M".
- The FN Karambit #387 must be described as "valued at / offered $1.5M, never sold".

### Gaps
- No cash-transfer proof (screenshots, escrow records) is publicly available for any six-figure deal.

## Q3. Open price-history pages: do they show seed-level sales without login?

### Takeaway
cs2pattern-prices.com publicly shows CSFloat-derived sales grouped by seed or pattern tier, which makes it the best open seed-level source found. Mainstream trackers (SteamAnalyst, csgoskins.gg, CSDB, HLTV skins) show wear-level medians and listings, not seed. Whether CSFloat's or BUFF's own pages show sales without login was not verified.

### Cited Findings
- cs2pattern-prices.com publishes per-seed/tier pages with recorded CSFloat sales: Five-SeveN CH blue gems (81 sales, top #278 at $2,156), Five-SeveN Heat Treated Tier 2 (top #406 at $159), M4A1-S Fade per seed (#441 99% at $5,677.14; #551 at $368), Talon MF F&I-4, Specialist Gloves Big Swell "max yellow" #132 — [blue-gems-1](https://cs2pattern-prices.com/weapons/five-seven/case-hardened/blue-gems-1), [heat-treated](https://cs2pattern-prices.com/weapons/five-seven/heat-treated/blue-gems-2), [M4A1-S 99/441](https://cs2pattern-prices.com/weapons/m4a1-s/fade/99/441), [M4A1-S 91/551](https://cs2pattern-prices.com/weapons/m4a1-s/fade/91/551), [Specialist Big Swell](https://cs2pattern-prices.com/weapons/specialist-gloves/big-swell/max-yellow/132).
- According to a vendor (cs2.sh), CSFloat's official API is listing-only and keeps no sales record. Its archive provides daily average sale price and volume from 2022 — [cs2.sh CSFloat API](https://cs2.sh/csfloat-api); [archive docs](https://cs2.sh/docs/archive-csfloat).
- Another vendor (CSPriceAPI) offers completed CSFloat sales from the last 72 hours with float, pattern seed and stickers. Older data is at a paid "Enterprise" tier — [cspriceapi](https://cspriceapi.com/csfloat-api).
- BUFF: a vendor reports real BUFF sale prices since 6 Sep 2024 by float range and fade range (fade % is the closest to pattern-level). The open-source BUFF archive stores listing counts per price point, not seeds — [cs2.sh buff](https://cs2.sh/buff-api); [atalantus archive](https://github.com/atalantus/buff-price-history-archive).
- CSDB shows 30-day sales tables by wear, e.g. Pandora's Box FN with no sales and MW median $5,388 — [CSDB](https://csdb.gg/skin/sport-gloves-pandora-s-box/).
- A Kaggle dataset of historical BUFF/CSFloat/Youpin prices exists (item-level) — [Kaggle](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/idomanteu/cs2-historical-item-prices-buff-csfloat-youpin).
- SteamAnalyst's fade guide cites CSFloat sales by fade-% bucket — [steamanalyst fade guide](https://www.steamanalyst.com/guides/fade).

### Inferences
- For the calculator site, cs2pattern-prices.com tier pages (Five-SeveN CH, Fade %, F&I) are the most practical openly linkable "real sales by seed" evidence. Disclose that its data source (CSFloat) is second-hand.

### Gaps
- I could not test in a browser whether csfloat.com item pages or buff.163.com show sales history while logged out (network blocked here).
- pattern.wiki (seed database) was blocked by the proxy, so it was not checked.

## Q4. Typical premium ratios stated by guides (ESTIMATES, not sales)

### Takeaway
Guides agree on ordering, but the multipliers vary widely. Ruby/Sapphire run about 2.5× to 8× Phase 2/4 depending on knife. Full fade adds roughly +15 to 40%. True Fire & Ice adds about 2× to 5× (some claim up to 10×). Centered Slaughter Diamond adds +25% to +300%. Case Hardened blue gems can run 100× or more over a same-float base. Always label these as estimates.

### Cited Findings
- **Doppler (estimates):**
  - "Ruby trades at multiples of Phase 2, often 3 to 8× depending on knife and float" — one of [cspriceapi Doppler guide](https://cspriceapi.com/resources/cs2-doppler-phases-price-guide) / [skin.club Doppler](https://skin.club/community/en/articles/doppler-phases) / [skin.land](https://skin.land/blog/csgocs2-knife-doppler-phases/) (**URL not pinned**).
  - SteamAnalyst FN listings imply about 2.5 to 3×: Ursus Ruby $664 vs P2 $259 (≈2.6×); Butterfly Ruby $10,254 vs P2 $4,117 (≈2.5×) — [Ursus](https://www.steamanalyst.com/skin/ursus-knife-doppler), [Butterfly](https://www.steamanalyst.com/skin/butterfly-knife-doppler).
  - One analysis: Ruby is 3.7 to 7.9× Phase 4 on Karambit/Butterfly/M9, but only 2.4× on Navaja (**URL not pinned**, same result set).
  - Phase 2 is "often 30 to 80% above Phase 1" (guide, URL not pinned).
  - Black Pearl is sometimes priced above Ruby: Paracord BP $637 vs Ruby $501; Skeleton BP $2,485 vs Ruby $2,181 (listings) — [Paracord](https://www.steamanalyst.com/skin/paracord-knife-doppler), [Skeleton](https://www.steamanalyst.com/skin/skeleton-knife-doppler).
  - Sapphire is "typically a step below" Ruby per one guide, but other guides disagree.
- **Fade %:**

### Inferences
- The reference should quote ranges with "estimate" labels and prefer CSFloat-sample-based figures (SteamAnalyst fade buckets, cs2pattern-prices medians) over blog multipliers.
- Ruby-to-Phase ratios are knife-dependent. Cheap knives run about 2.4 to 3×, premium knives up to about 8×. A single global multiplier would mislead.

### Gaps
- No sales-based multiplier dataset was found for Doppler phases, F&I, Slaughter or Crimson Web. All multipliers come from guides of unknown methodology and snapshot date.
- No glove seed premiums (e.g., Sport/Specialist "max" patterns) were documented beyond the existence of cs2pattern-prices tier pages.
