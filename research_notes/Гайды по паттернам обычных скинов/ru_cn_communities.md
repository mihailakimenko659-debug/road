# RU and CN community sources on special patterns (seeds) for ordinary CS2 gun skins

Method and limits (read first):
- Research date: 2026-10-19. Every WebFetch failed with DNS errors (`getaddrinfo ENOTFOUND` for buff.163.com, esports.ru, csgo2.wiki), so **no page was opened directly**. Everything below comes from WebSearch result snippets and the search engine's summaries of them. Seed lists can be cut short in snippets, and nothing here has been checked against the full page. Before a parser relies on any of it, open the page and confirm.
- Date notes like "~784 days ago" come from the search tool's relative-age metadata. They are not publication dates I read on the page.
- I found **no BUFF163 tag or filter names** for ordinary-skin special templates. BUFF's goods pages could not be loaded, and the snippets never named a "特殊模板" tag. See Gaps.
- Wear: no snippet linked a seed claim to a wear range, except where noted.
- CN→EN name mapping: entries marked "tentative" are my own mapping and were not confirmed in any source.

## Q1. Which ordinary gun skins have documented special templates in CN sources, and which seeds?

### Takeaway
Seed lists for ordinary guns in CN sources come mainly from three places: BUFF163's editorial "饰品杂谈" articles, the community tier site CSGO2.WIKI (csgoob.cn), and older Steam, bilibili and Tieba guides. The skins covered are AWP | PAW, UMP-45 | Minotaur's Labyrinth, XM1014 | Seasons, a Five-SeveN floral skin, Dual Berettas "LOL", AK-47 | Case Hardened, Desert Eagle | Heat Treated and Five-SeveN Heat Treated/Case Hardened. I found no CN source that tags special templates for Printstream, Asiimov, Fire Serpent or Empress.

### Cited Findings
- **BUFF163 news 56151, "饰品杂谈：不止"全蓝"！小众模板彩蛋枪皮大全"** (~784 days old per search metadata, so roughly Aug–Sep 2024). It covers niche "easter-egg" templates beyond Case Hardened, mostly on SMGs, shotguns and pistols, with few rifles — [BUFF163](https://buff.163.com/news/56151)
  - AWP | 猫猫狗狗 (AWP | PAW): "招财猫" (lucky / golden cat) templates **41, 350**. "金手雷" (golden grenade) templates **429, 926**. "持刀猫" (cat holding a knife) templates **212, 369, 628, 737** — [BUFF163](https://buff.163.com/news/56151)
  - UMP-45 | 弥诺陶洛斯迷宫 (UMP-45 | Minotaur's Labyrinth): template **513** puts the Minotaur from the maze centre at the centre of the gun. A star also appears on the slide/strap area in special templates — [BUFF163](https://buff.163.com/news/56151)
  - XM1014 | 四季 (XM1014 | Seasons): only special templates show the "蓝叶" (blue leaf) from the far left of the texture. **No seeds visible in the snippet** — [BUFF163](https://buff.163.com/news/56151)
  - FN57 | 紫藤老树 (Five-SeveN, EN name tentative: possibly "Withered Vine"): special templates show a "红花" (red flower) that is otherwise hidden. **Seeds not visible** — [BUFF163](https://buff.163.com/news/56151)
  - 双枪 | 暗网总机 "LOL" (Dual Berettas, EN name tentative: possibly "Switch Board"): template **80** — [BUFF163](https://buff.163.com/news/56151). A later search could not reconfirm this item from the snippet.
- **XM1014 | Seasons blue-leaf seeds** (CN Steam guide "csgo玄学皮肤科普", old CS:GO era, ~7 years per search metadata): **130, 182, 26, 234, 283, 286, 338, 390, 442, 546, 598, 605, 650, 659, 78, 955**. A centred blue leaf is preferred. Seeds may have shifted after CS2 — [Steam guide 1923372529](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=1923372529)
- **Desert Eagle | 热处理 (Desert Eagle | Heat Treated)**, CSGO2.WIKI tier list scored 0–16 by community members, tiers T0–T4. T0 = full blue with a standout feature. **843** ranks #1 (largest and cleanest blue on the slide serrations, even blue/purple split). **66 and 347** tie for #2. **308** is also T0. T2 includes **148, 159, 587** — [csgoob.cn tier list](https://www.csgoob.cn/article/rechuli_shaying); mirror at [csgo2.wiki](https://www.csgo2.wiki/article/rechuli_shaying)
  - A companion article (slug dated 2024-10-12) gives survivor counts and wear distribution for T0/T1 Heat Treated Deagles, with sample wear values and prices for 843. **The figures were not visible in the snippet** — [csgoob.cn](https://www.csgoob.cn/article/shaying_rechuli_2024_10_12)
  - Background: Desert Eagle | Heat Treated is an Armory-pass item released in early October 2024 — [cs2codes.cn](https://cs2codes.cn/%E6%B8%B8%E6%88%8F%E6%8C%87%E5%8D%97/%E6%B2%99%E6%BC%A0%E4%B9%8B%E9%B9%B0-%E7%83%AD%E5%A4%84%E7%90%86-cs2-%E6%8C%87%E5%8D%97%E4%B8%8E%E5%9B%BE%E6%A1%88)
- **FN57 热处理 special-template list** (reposted; the site itself says the content is unverified). **Seeds not visible.** The EN name is ambiguous: it could be Five-SeveN | Case Hardened, or a Heat Treated variant if one exists. Verify — [csgoob.cn](https://www.csgoob.cn/article/rechuli_fn57)
- **AK-47 | 表面淬火 (AK-47 | Case Hardened) "蓝顶" (blue top)**:
  - CSGO2.WIKI repost: "大蓝顶" (large blue top) **868, 823, 935, 844, 888, 996, 905**. Separate lists exist for half and small blue tops, but those seeds are not visible — [csgoob.cn](https://www.csgoob.cn/article/ak-47_case_hardened)
  - cs2codes.cn: top templates **661, 151, 955, 321, 387, 670, 179**, with 661 the most valuable — [cs2codes.cn](https://cs2codes.cn/%E6%B8%B8%E6%88%8F%E6%8C%87%E5%8D%97/ak-47-%E6%B7%AC%E7%81%AB%E8%93%9D%E5%AE%9D%E7%9F%B3%EF%BC%9A%E7%A7%8D%E5%AD%90%E6%A8%A1%E6%9D%BF%E5%85%A8%E6%8C%87%E5%8D%97)
  - 18183.com (Sept 2022): T1–T4 grading. T1 means at least 50% of the top is solid blue. Full-blue recommendations **661, 670, 321, 955, 179, 151**. Full-blue-top templates are said to be about 1% of all templates — [18183](https://www.18183.com/xinwen/202209/4138954.html), [18183](https://www.18183.com/xinwen/202209/4138887.html)
  - Gold-top AK Case Hardened seeds are in a bilibili video; **seeds not visible** — [bilibili BV1bA4m1P79G](https://www.bilibili.com/video/BV1bA4m1P79G/)
  - "13 good-value blue-top templates" video; **seeds not visible** — [bilibili BV1ar4y1B7KJ](https://www.bilibili.com/video/BV1ar4y1B7KJ/)
- A Chinese-language page attributes **Five-SeveN #278** and **MAC-10 #667** as notable "full-colour" patterns. The search summary did not say which page made this claim; the nearest candidates are community.skin.club articles. **Low confidence; unattributed** — [skin.club CS2 pattern index guide](https://community.skin.club/en/articles/cs2-skin-index)
- **Glock-18 / MAC-10 | 渐变之色 (Fade)**: BUFF "pro inventory" articles show individual items, e.g. MAC-10 Fade FN 0.04 seed 907, FN 0.006 seed 827; Glock-18 Fade FN 0.01 seed 502, FN 0.007 seed 746. **These are owned examples, not special-template claims** — [BUFF163 pro-inventory series, e.g.](https://buff.163.com/news/57872)
- For Fade skins, the CN "玄学" (pattern-luck) value lies in the purple percentage — [bilibili cv2672488](https://www.bilibili.com/read/cv2672488/)
- Other CN pattern-luck lists name FN57 | 神祗 (Five-SeveN | Kami), where the luck sits on the rear of the gun on the side shown when held. **No seeds visible** — [Zhihu q/53853488](https://www.zhihu.com/question/53853488)
- bilibili UP 雷恩 has a three-part series "【CS2皮肤搭配】这些有特殊模板的饰品推荐 (上/中/下)". The description says templates are marked in the video, but **no seeds appear in the text** — [bilibili BV1FUp3eJEBy](https://www.bilibili.com/video/BV1FUp3eJEBy/)
- Old CN pattern-luck compilations, not opened, no seeds visible: [bilibili cv4454041](https://www.bilibili.com/read/cv4454041), [Tieba 5036812741](https://tieba.baidu.com/p/5036812741), [Zhihu q/378974314](https://www.zhihu.com/question/378974314), [Hupu 皮肤控 #45](https://m.hupu.com/bbs/28566923.html), [C5GAME wiki AK CH](https://www.c5game.com/wiki/541573165.html)

### Inferences
- In CN sources, "特殊模板" for ordinary guns mostly means two things. Easter-egg placement seeds: PAW golden cat, Minotaur 513, Seasons blue leaf, Five-SeveN red flower. Blue-coverage tiers on Case Hardened / Heat Treated skins: AK CH, Deagle Heat Treated, Five-SeveN. The skins the brief guessed at (Printstream, Asiimov, Fire Serpent, Empress) turned up no template claims in my searches. Treat them as unconfirmed.
- CSGO2.WIKI (csgoob.cn / csgo2.wiki) is the most structured CN tier source, with numeric scores, tiers and survivor statistics. It is the best target for a parser seed list once it can be fetched.

### Gaps
- BUFF163 "特殊模板" or pattern-tag filter names for ordinary skins: not found. goods pages and filters were unreachable.
- SteamDT, C5GAME, IGXE, youpin898, NGA and xiaoheihe template tags: no snippets with tag names or seeds.
- Seeds for XM1014 Seasons in CS2 (only the CS:GO-era list was found), the Five-SeveN red flower, Five-SeveN Heat Treated/CH, and the half/small blue top AK lists.
- CN names "满印" and "极品模板": no ordinary-skin seed lists appeared.

## Q2. Which ordinary gun skins have special-pattern claims in RU sources, and which seeds?

### Takeaway
The main RU sources are the cs.money RU blog, the lis-skins RU blog (2026), esports.ru, cyber.sports.ru, skinout.gg/ru and older RU Steam guides. They repeat the global canon: AK CH Blue Gem, AWP PAW / Electric Hive, Desert Eagle Hypnotic "zebra eye", P2000 clover, P2000 Granite Marbleized "hamster", Galil and Tec-9 Sandstorm, Glock Fade/Moonrise, Five-SeveN Kami, MAC-10 Last Dive and UMP-45 Minotaur. I found nothing in VK, Dzen, Habr or Telegram beyond news items.

### Cited Findings
- esports.ru "Редкие паттерны CS2: список номеров…" (~271 days old per metadata, so about Jan 2026): **P2000 | Протравленный** seed **355** listed first (with 107, 269 and others). **Desert Eagle | Гипноз** (Hypnotic) "Глаз зебры" (zebra eye) includes **302** — [esports.ru](https://esports.ru/counter-strike-2/articles/redkie-patterny-skinov-v-cs2/)
  - An older RU Steam guide lists "Весь глаз зебры" (whole zebra eye) as **12, 86, 104, 245, 302, …** (list truncated) — [Steam guide 2429014707](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2429014707)
  - A P2000 "Протравленный" clover-leaf guide includes 355 — [Steam guide 2797232553](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2797232553). The EN name is tentative: probably **P2000 | Acid Etched**. Verify.
  - esports.ru also has a Desert Eagle Heat Treated rare-pattern article; **seeds not visible** — [esports.ru](https://esports.ru/counter-strike-2/articles/vse-redkie-pattreny-desert-eagle-termicheskaya-obrabotka-v-cs2/)
- cs.money RU blog "Топ редких паттернов на скинах в CS2": **P2000 | Granite Marbleized** seed **125** shows a hamster face on the rear of the pistol. The blog says a rare pattern can raise AK CH by an order of magnitude but does not affect M4A1-S VariCamo — [cs.money RU](https://cs.money/ru/blog/skiny/redkie-krutye-patterny-na-skinah/)
- cs.money RU AWP article: **AWP | PAW Golden Cat** seeds **41, 350, 19, 35, 177, 531, 581, 624, 675, 744** (the snippet lists 41 twice). **AWP | Electric Hive Max Blue 273, 436, 902**. Max Orange includes **253, 54**. It also covers AWP Fade (412 for 100%), BOOM and POP AWP — [cs.money RU AWP](https://cs.money/ru/blog/skiny/skiny-awp-s-redkimi-patternami-fade-boom-electric-hive-i-drugie/)
- skinout.gg/ru names the PAW variants "Золотая кошка" (golden cat), "Глаза-дырки" (hollow eyes) and a cat holding a grenade, and gives AK CH legendary seed 661 — [skinout.gg/ru](https://skinout.gg/ru/blog/riedkiie-pattierny-skinov-ks-gho/)
- cs.money RU (via the general search summary) names these as popular rare-pattern ordinary skins: AWP PAW, MAC-10 Last Dive, UMP-45 Minotaur's Labyrinth, Five-SeveN Kami, Glock-18 Moonrise, Galil AR Sandstorm, Tec-9 Sandstorm and Five-SeveN Neon Kimono. **Seeds not visible for most** — [cs.money RU full guide](https://cs.money/ru/blog/skiny/polnyj-gajd-po-patternam-skinov-csgo/)
- lis-skins RU "Редкие паттерны в CS2 в 2026 году: гид по индексам" has sections on Glock-18 Fade, Glock-18 Moonrise and Galil AR Sandstorm. **Section seeds not visible** — [lis-skins](https://lis-skins.com/blog/redkie-patterny-v-cs2/)
- lis-skins AK-47 Case Hardened tier list: Blue Gem T1 **151, 955, 321, 387, 670, 179**; T2 **592, 4, 905, 13, 168, 429**; reference seed **661** — [lis-skins](https://lis-skins.com/blog/ak-47-case-hardened-pattern-guide-case-hardened-tier-list/)
- cs.money RU AK CH rare-pattern article exists; **seeds not visible** — [cs.money RU](https://cs.money/ru/blog/skiny/redkie-patterny-ak-47-case-handered/)
- Other RU explainer and list pages, no new seeds visible: [cyber.sports.ru](https://cyber.sports.ru/cs/blogs/3400892.html), [cs-config.com](https://cs-config.com/guides/counter-strike-2/pattern-skinov-v-cs2), [Steam RU guide 2289225749](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2289225749)
- Not RU, but surfaced by RU queries (English sources): **Five-SeveN | Kami** "909" is the meme seed, with sales reported above $1,400. 590 and 662 are top tier, 657 "Big Panda", 628 "The Watcher" — [skin.land](https://skin.land/blog/best-five-seven-kami-patterns/), [profilerr](https://profilerr.net/cs2-five-seven-kami-patterns/). **Galil AR | Sandstorm 583** is almost fully purple. A purple list adds 761, 739, 178 — [cs.money EN](https://cs.money/blog/cs-go-skins/rare-cool-skin-patterns/), [Steam guide 3387158441](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3387158441). Galil AR | Kami 761, 620, 888 — [profilerr](https://profilerr.net/cs2-galil-kami-patterns/)

### Inferences
- RU marketplace blogs (cs.money, lis-skins) are the main RU-language seed sources. The communities (VK, Telegram, Dzen) did not show up as primary seed documenters in web search. They probably repost these lists.
- Overlap with CN: AWP PAW golden cat 41/350 and AK CH 661/151/955/321/387/670/179 match across RU, CN and EN sources. That raises confidence in those seeds.

### Gaps
- VK, Dzen, Telegram, YouTube RU and market.csgo.com blog: no indexed pages with seed lists found.
- Full seed lists for Glock Moonrise, MAC-10 Last Dive, Tec-9 Sandstorm, Five-SeveN Neon Kimono, Desert Eagle Hypnotic (complete) and P2000 clover (complete): truncated or not visible.

## Q3. Evidence of premiums (price for a special template vs. a regular copy)

### Takeaway
Hard premium figures with dates are scarce in what the snippets showed. Most sources state premiums only qualitatively. The quantitative data points found are an AK CH 661 trade, a CN video headline of a 1.7M CNY BUFF sale, a Five-SeveN Kami 909 sale figure, and a regular Glock Fade baseline. None is a recent BUFF tagged-vs-untagged comparison.

### Cited Findings
- lis-skins (2026): copies with a favourable pattern index sell above ordinary copies of the same wear, "иногда в несколько раз" (sometimes several times over). An ordinary Glock-18 | Fade is about **$1,350**; mostly-purple copies sell notably higher (no figure) — [lis-skins](https://lis-skins.com/blog/redkie-patterny-v-cs2/)
- cs.money RU: a rare pattern can raise AK-47 CH by an order of magnitude but has no effect on M4A1-S VariCamo. The Steam Market does not price rare patterns — [cs.money RU](https://cs.money/ru/blog/skiny/redkie-krutye-patterny-na-skinah/)
- AK-47 CH 661 with 4 Titan holo stickers reportedly sold for **$500,000** (~3.435M CNY), reported as the second-biggest CS:GO trade. Date not visible — [bubaigei.com](https://bubaigei.com/game-news/564)
- A bilibili video title mentions "buff12月3日170w成交的661淬火": a 661 Case Hardened sold on BUFF on 3 December for 1.7M CNY. **Year not visible** — [bilibili BV1ar4y1B7KJ](https://www.bilibili.com/video/BV1ar4y1B7KJ/)
- AK CH: full blue top is said to be about 1% of templates (2022) — [18183](https://www.18183.com/xinwen/202209/4138887.html)
- Five-SeveN Kami 909: sales "sometimes exceeded $1,400" (EN source, undated in snippet) — [skin.land](https://skin.land/blog/best-five-seven-kami-patterns/)
- Desert Eagle Heat Treated: the CSGO2.WIKI 2024-10-12 article contains price samples for 843 copies, **not visible**. Regular baseline snapshots: IGXE FN about ¥388.88, MW about ¥49.44, FT about ¥25 (snapshot date unknown) — [csgoob.cn](https://www.csgoob.cn/article/shaying_rechuli_2024_10_12), [IGXE](https://www.igxe.cn/product/730/685501)
- AWP PAW regular baselines for comparison: MW about ¥17.01 on BUFF, ¥24.41 on Steam. StatTrak FN about ¥95.44 on IGXE. Snapshot dates unknown; no golden-cat price found — [jbskins](https://www.jbskins.com/skins/detail/8464.html), [IGXE](https://www.igxe.cn/product/730/614140)

### Inferences
- For a parser, the measurable premium has to come from live BUFF / youpin / CSFloat listings filtered by paintseed. The editorial sources give only qualitative premiums or one-off sales.

### Gaps
- No dated BUFF price for a tagged template vs. an untagged copy, for any ordinary skin.
- No premium data for PAW golden cat, Minotaur 513, Seasons blue leaf, Deagle Heat Treated 843, or Hypnotic zebra eye.
