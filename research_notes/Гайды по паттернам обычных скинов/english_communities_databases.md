# English-language (non-Steam) communities and databases documenting special seeds on ordinary CS2 gun skins

Research date: 2026-10-19. Method and access limits (matter for how far you can trust these notes):
- WebFetch could not load pages: csdb.gg and pattern.wiki failed with DNS errors (`getaddrinfo ENOTFOUND`). Every finding below comes from **WebSearch result snippets and their summaries**. The search tool's own summarizer sometimes merges sources, so specific seed numbers need checking against the live page.
- **Reddit cannot be reached.** The search API rejects `reddit.com` ("domains are not accessible to our user agent"), and pages do not load. So no r/csgomarketforum, r/GlobalOffensiveTrade, r/csgo or r/cs2 threads were seen.
- **GitHub search was blocked.** `gh search repos` returned HTTP 403 because the session is limited to its configured repositories. No open JSON seed datasets were found by web search either.
- Twitter/X and Discord: searches turned up nothing usable.
- Steam Workshop guides appear in results and are cited only where they help cross-check a non-Steam source (they are outside the assignment's scope).
- **Scope:** only regular guns (not knives or gloves). Case Hardened, Heat Treated, Fade and Gamma Doppler on guns count as ordinary gun skins.

## Which regular weapon skins have community-recognized special seeds (feature, seeds, source, date)?

### Takeaway
Documented, seed-specific premiums on regular guns cluster in a few families. The first is the **gradient/marble finishes**: AK-47, Five-SeveN and MAC-10 Case Hardened; Desert Eagle and Five-SeveN Heat Treated; Glock-18, AWP and MAC-10 Fade; Glock-18 Gamma Doppler Emerald. The second is **"motif" skins**, where a seed places or hides a picture element: AWP | PAW, Glock-18 | Moonrise star, XM1014 | Seasons blue leaf, Glock-18 | Grinder full black. Several skins named in the brief have **no seed variation**, according to csgoskins.gg/cs.money item pages: AK-47 | Head Shot, M4A1-S | Printstream, AWP | Chromatic Aberration, AK-47 | Nightwish, USP-S | Kill Confirmed, SSG 08 | Dragonfire, MP9 | Starlight Protector and Five-SeveN | Hybrid. A parser should treat any "special seed" claim for these as a red flag.

### Inferences
- The "special seed" premium on regular guns is real and well documented mainly for the Case Hardened, Heat Treated, Fade and Gamma Doppler families, plus a short list of motif skins (PAW, Moonrise, Seasons, Grinder, Aphrodite). For most popular Coverts (Printstream, Nightwish, Head Shot, Chromatic Aberration, Kill Confirmed, Dragonfire, Starlight Protector), "rare seed" talk is either unsupported or comes from low-quality sources.
- For a parser, the csgoskins.gg per-item "Pattern Variants: Yes/No" flag is a cheap first filter to drop no-variation skins.
- Seed lists are near-copies across marketplace blogs (Hotspawn, shadowpay, hellcase, pricempire, csgoskins.gg), so agreement between them is not independent confirmation.

### Gaps
- No Reddit, Twitter/X or Discord content could be seen (reddit.com is blocked for search and fetch). Community-only claims (e.g. "Head Shot max", Five-SeveN Hybrid) could not be checked at their origin.
- UMP-45, MP7 and R8 Fade, AWP BOOM, AUG Akihabara Accept, AK-47 Fire Serpent and Crimson Web on guns: no seeds visible.
- The MAC-10 Fade seed list is identical to the Glock Fade list, and the Five-SeveN Heat Treated top three overlap with the Desert Eagle Heat Treated list. Both need verification on the live pages.
- No CSFloat blog post on regular-skin patterns was found. pattern.wiki and csdb.gg could not be opened to read their full lists.

## Which databases list the most regular-skin patterns (coverage) and their visible terms; any data on premium by seed group?

### Takeaway
By declared coverage, **Dispattern** is the largest community special-pattern database: 1,209 tracked patterned skins and 1,358 user-submitted special patterns. **pattern.wiki** shows all ~1,000 seeds per pattern-relevant skin, with sortable metrics such as blue leaf, rotation and offset. **cs.money wiki** says more than 100 CS2 skins are pattern-dependent. **csdb.gg** is a curated gallery plus guides. No source surfaced real **median-sale-price-by-seed** data. Premium claims are blog estimates, except the documented AK-47 CH #661 StatTrak FN sale.

### Cited Findings
- **Dispattern:** "453 analyses, 1,209 tracked patterned skins, 1,358 submitted special patterns". Analyzable finishes are scored by software, and the community submits "special patterns" (hearts, faces, animals). Page URLs follow /WEAPONS/[weapon]/[skin]. **Terms visible:** submitting requires Steam sign-in, and users are asked to box only the detail itself. — [dispattern.com](https://dispattern.com/); [AWP Exothermic page](https://dispattern.com/WEAPONS/AWP/AWP_EXOTHERMIC); [blog](https://dispattern.com/blog/disthispattern-is-amazing)
- **pattern.wiki:** describes itself as a "CS2 Skin Pattern Checker & Database" and "shows all 1,000 seed variations for skins with unique patterns". Pages are per skin (e.g. Glock Fade /100, Glock GD Emerald, XM1014 Seasons sorted by `xm_blue_leaf`), plus a skin index by weapon. The site says seeds run 0–1000. — [pattern.wiki](https://pattern.wiki/); [skins index](https://pattern.wiki/skins/); [AWP skins](https://pattern.wiki/skins/weapon_awp/); [about (2022)](https://pattern.wiki/wiki/about/)
- **csdb.gg:** pattern/seed lookup plus a gallery of "most sought-after" seeds. Main finishes covered are Case Hardened, Fade, Marble Fade and Crimson Web. It also has the "all CS2 rare skin patterns full list" page, per-weapon blue-gem pages and a Glock Grinder guide. A CSFloat API integration is "planned". Seeds are described as 0–999. — [csdb.gg pattern database](https://csdb.gg/pattern-database/); [all rare patterns](https://csdb.gg/all-cs2-rare-skin-patterns-full-list); [blog/patterns](https://csdb.gg/blog/patterns); [blue-gem](https://csdb.gg/blue-gem/)
- **wiki.cs.money/rare-patterns:** the list of pattern-dependent CS2 skins "has now exceeded 100", with per-skin wiki pages. — [wiki.cs.money rare patterns](https://wiki.cs.money/rare-patterns); [cs.money full patterns guide](https://cs.money/blog/cs-go-skins/full-csgo-patterns-guide/)
- **Hotspawn "All Pattern-Based CS2 Skins":** the visible part lists AK-47 (10 skins, e.g. Case Hardened, Predator, Jungle Spray), AUG (6, e.g. Snake Pit, Surveillance), AWP (14, e.g. Acheron, BOOM, Fade, PAW), CZ75 (5, e.g. Polymer, Twist) and Desert Eagle (17+, e.g. Heat Treated, Meteorite, Mudder). The rest was cut off. The page was updated about 87 days before the search. — [Hotspawn](https://www.hotspawn.com/counter-strike/news/all-pattern-based-cs2-skins)
- **cs2skins.gg/pattern:** a pattern browser with per-weapon counts (Glock-18 57, P250 57). It is unclear whether these count only pattern-relevant skins. — [cs2skins.gg](https://cs2skins.gg/pattern)
- **Steam Analyst pattern checker:** a tier table with confidence labels ("well established" vs. "unconfirmed — verify before quoting"). — [steamanalyst](https://www.steamanalyst.com/tools/pattern-checker)
- **cs2.io:** a Case Hardened tier price ladder "on top of base price". It gives AK-47 Tier 1 as $153–$378, which looks inconsistent with known blue-gem sales. Possibly a premium figure for low-wear tiers; treat as suspect. — [cs2.io case hardened](https://cs2.io/en/skins/patterns/case-hardened)
- **Pricempire guides:** a CS2 pattern guide, AWP Fade, MAC-10 Fade and MAC-10 Amber Fade. — [pricempire pattern guide](https://pricempire.com/guides/cs2-pattern-guide)
- **csgoskins.gg:** item pages carry a "Pattern Variants: Yes/No" flag, plus blog seed guides (Glock, AWP and MAC-10 Fade; PAW; blue gems). — [example](https://csgoskins.gg/items/m4a1-s-fade)
- Other mirror sites: [wiki.csgoluck.com pattern database](https://wiki.csgoluck.com/pattern-database/); [steamdb.com blue gem page](https://steamdb.com/en/skins/cs2/cs2-blue-gem-skins). That page is on steamdb.com, not steamdb.info; it was not established whether the two are affiliated. No steamdb.info/patterns page was found.
- **CSFloat:** no CSFloat blog post on regular-skin patterns surfaced. Third parties say CSFloat lets you filter listings by paint seed and shows the paint seed without extensions. — [cs2floatchecker](https://cs2floatchecker.com/blog/pattern-guide)
- **Documented sale (premium evidence):** a StatTrak FN AK-47 Case Hardened #661 sold on 2024-06-05. The price was undisclosed but "confirmed between $1M and $1.5M", and the sale was brokered by roflm0nster and zipel. An earlier MW #661 sold for $400k (sources say 2022 or 2023). — [skin.club news](https://community.skin.club/en/news/cs2-ak-47-skin-sells-for-record-breaking-1-million); [Gfinity](https://www.gfinityesports.com/news/disgusting-counter-strike-skin-sells-for-way-too-much-money); [skin.land](https://skin.land/blog/ak-47-case-hardened-661-most-expensive-skin/)
- **Premium magnitudes (blog claims, not sales data):** blue gems "10–100x" or "10x–1000x" base price. Extreme prices apply only to blue-dominant seeds, and most copies trade at normal wear price. — [cs2.io](https://cs2.io/en/skins/patterns/case-hardened); [steamanalyst AK CH guide](https://www.steamanalyst.com/guides/ak47-case-hardened)
- **Evidence of a discount for near-max fades:** the 99% "fake full fade" AWP sells "significantly less" than 100%. — [pricempire AWP Fade](https://pricempire.com/guides/awp-fade-guide)

### Inferences
- For a market parser, the best seed sources are pattern.wiki (full seed tables) and Dispattern (community motif submissions), cross-checked against csgoskins.gg and Hotspawn lists. Premiums have to be computed from raw sales (e.g. CSFloat filtered by paint seed), because no public per-seed median dataset was found.

### Gaps
- No median-sale-price-by-seed data, with counts and dates, was found for any regular gun skin.
- Terms of use and API terms for pattern.wiki, csdb.gg and csgoskins.gg could not be read, since their pages would not load. Only Dispattern's submission rule (Steam login) was visible.
- No open GitHub dataset was found; GitHub search was blocked (403).
