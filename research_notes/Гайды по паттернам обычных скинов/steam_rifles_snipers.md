# Steam Community guides on paint seed / pattern templates for regular CS2 rifle and sniper skins (AK-47, M4A4, M4A1-S, AWP, SSG 08, SG 553, AUG, FAMAS, Galil AR, SCAR-20, G3SG1)

**Method and data-quality note (applies to everything below).** The environment's proxy blocks steamcommunity.com: WebFetch failed with DNS ENOTFOUND, curl got a 403 on CONNECT, and web.archive.org is blocked too. No guide page was opened directly. Everything here comes from WebSearch result snippets and the search tool's own summaries of them, collected on 2026-10-19. The query form that worked best was `"Steam Community :: Guide" <weapon> pattern` in extended mode.

Snippets are often cut off mid-list, so every seed list below should be treated as partial unless it is marked "full". Many guides carry a Steam banner saying "removed from the community because it violates Steam Community & Content Guidelines" and/or "incompatible with Counter-Strike 2". The search index still has their text cached. Ages such as "~393 days old" are what the snippet showed relative to the search date, and may reflect the last update rather than the first publication.

## 1. Guide catalog: URL, title, author, date, language

### Takeaway
I found 50+ Steam guides about seed-dependent looks on regular rifle and sniper skins. Most of them are from 2024–2026, and many are by the prolific author **Hartwich** (the "CSPatterns Guides" hub, id 3300003211) and by **blu** (the "CS2 Pattern Guides List" hub, id 3601330547). The user's example, **id 3550106561, is "CS2: M4A1-S", a Russian-language guide to M4A1-S | Solitude** with an overpay-multiplier table.

### Inferences
- The Steam search index works around the access block: cached guide text is still served by search even for guides Steam has hidden. The removal banners seem widespread across Hartwich and blu guides. The common pattern is a trade-offer and site-promotion link in the guide, which the snippets showed mixed into Hartwich's Boom text. This is my inference, not confirmed. A parser cannot count on these URLs staying viewable.
- 2025–2026 Limited and "Armory" releases quickly get seed guides: M4A1-S Solitude got 8+ guides within days of its Aug 2025 release, and Fatal Glitch got one in July 2026. These items are the most likely to show a real market premium soon after release.

### Gaps
- I could not open any guide page, so authors, dates, and full seed lists are incomplete for most entries.
- Author names were not visible for 3550106561, the AK-47 Aphrodite guide (3651356503), the Solitude guides other than those noted, and most SSG 08 guides.

## 2. Exact skin, feature, seeds, wear visibility

### Takeaway
The seed-dependent features with concrete numbers visible in the snippets fall into these groups:
- placement of a sun or character: Solitude, Daybreak, Silk Tiger, Paw, Acheron skulls
- text visibility: POP AWP, Fizzy POP, Boom, Printstream "Lo-|/e"
- colour coverage or "max colour": Converter red mag, Urban DDPAT white, Sandstorm purple/white, Night Camo blue, Electric Hive max blue/orange, Aphrodite gems, Redline MAX RED, Glitched Paint saturation, Fatal Glitch green/orange
- geometric formations: Exothermic diamond/pyramid
- fade percentage: AWP, M4A1-S, and SSG 08 Fades
- high-float cleanliness: Atheris, Acheron BS

For several examples the user named (Nightwish, Head Shot, Neon Rider, Temukau, Emphorosaur-S, Phantom Disruptor, Duality, Chromatic Aberration) **I found no Steam seed guide**, and third-party databases state the pattern index has no effect on those finishes.

## 3. Claimed price premium and evidence of sales

### Takeaway
Very few Steam guides claim a specific premium. The notable exception is **3550106561 (M4A1-S | Solitude)**, which gives explicit FN multipliers: Tier 1 3–6× (ideal up to 12×), Tier 2 1.5–3×, Tier 3 1.1–1.8×, and no-sun patterns ~1.0–1.25× over a $200 base. No guide snippet showed actual sale records. Several guides explicitly say premiums are small.

### Cited Findings
- **M4A1-S | Solitude, guide 3550106561.** The FN overpay table uses a $200 base, described as "the regular FN price on the day trading opened", and tells readers to adjust to the market.
  - Tier 1 (centered yellow or double sun): 3–6×, ideal up to 12×
  - Tier 2: 1.5–3×
  - Tier 3: 1.1–1.8×
  - No sun (blue over green): ~1.0–1.25×
  - Source: [CS2: M4A1-S](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3550106561)
- CS.MONEY quotes the same "3–6× (ideal up to 12×)" multiplier for FN Tier 1 yellow-sun Solitude. It is likely derived from the same source. — summary of [CS.MONEY on X](https://x.com/csmoneytrade/status/1958155703538868236)
- Third-party price claims for Solitude conflict. Skin.club says premium Golden Mountains and Red Sun copies go for ~$160–$1,000. Esportfire says Golden Mountains run from $140 (FT) to $1,040 (FN). Skin.land puts a regular FN at about $20. — [skin.club](https://community.skin.club/en/collections/m4a1s-solitude-pattern-guide); [Esportfire](https://esportfire.com/article/the-best-m4a1-s-solitude-patterns-in-cs2-16092025); [skin.land](https://skin.land/blog/the-m4a1s-solitude-pattern-guide/)
- **M4A1-S | Fizzy POP.** The guide says the horizontal-line patterns 403, 468, 961 "come with some overpay". No amount is given. — [3562167852](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3562167852)
- **SSG 08 | Abyss.** sacLi: "This is not a price guide… The patterns do not affect the price too much because it is a cheap skin." — [2873291788](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2873291788)
- **AWP | PAW.** A third party says even Tier 1 Gold Cat and Stoner Cat copies, which are 4 of 1000 seeds, sell for "only a few dollars more than the regular price". Another site puts Tier 1 Gold Cat at $20–$30 or more. The two conflict. — summary of [profilerr](https://profilerr.net/awp-paw-golden-cat-patterns/) and [lis-skins](https://lis-skins.com/blog/the-awp-paw-patterns-guide/)
- **AWP | Printstream.** No price was found for the "Lo-|/e" variant specifically. General BS prices are ~$44–51. — [csgoskins.gg](https://csgoskins.gg/items/awp-printstream)
- **AK-47 | Redline.** A third party says Redline "does not have significant pattern premium", which contradicts the MAX RED guide's premise. — summary of [csgoskins.gg](https://csgoskins.gg/items/ak-47-redline)
- **AK-47 | Case Hardened**, for context only: CSBoard says Tier 1 copies such as #661 can exceed $10,000 in FT. — [CSBoard](https://csboard.com/en/blog/ak-47-case-hardened-tier-list-all-4-tiers-best-patterns-for-2026)

### Inferences
- Apart from Fades and Case Hardened, Steam guides mostly rank how a seed looks, not what it sells for. The claimed premiums are unquantified or modest. The Solitude multipliers are the only explicit and testable claim, which makes them a priority check for the market parser.
- Many Hartwich and blu guides carry "removed" banners. This suggests guide authors use them to promote trading sites, which is a mild conflict of interest that can inflate rarity claims. This is my inference.

### Gaps
- No sale records or price histories appeared in any Steam guide snippet. The market parser must verify every premium claim against actual sales.
- I found no quantified premium claims for Daybreak, Converter, Urban DDPAT, Sandstorm, Night Camo, Exothermic, Atheris, Silk Tiger, Boom, Electric Hive, Aphrodite, Breakthrough, or Fatal Glitch.
